For the IaC checkpoint, define the storage account in your own repo (Bicep or Terraform) with **your** account name (the one `bw deploy` printed). Set `allowBlobPublicAccess: false` and `allowSharedKeyAccess: false`. Then check your incident note against `rubrics/incident-note.md`: it asks for the exposure window, what the token could access, how you revoked it, and how you know it worked.
