Two separate settings make the build log public: one on the **account** (`allowBlobPublicAccess`) and one on the **container** (its public access level). Turning off the account-level one overrides every container. To prevent account-key SAS tokens entirely, look at `allowSharedKeyAccess`, and at the **user delegation SAS**, which is signed with Entra credentials and is what you should issue for the account-alive checkpoint:
`az storage blob generate-sas --as-user --auth-mode login --permissions r --expiry <now + 60 min> …`
