Most findings are **platform settings** on each app, not code. In the portal, look at **Settings > Configuration > General settings** (HTTPS Only, FTP state, minimum TLS, SCM and FTP Basic Auth Publishing Credentials) and **API > CORS**. In Bicep they're `httpsOnly`, `siteConfig.ftpsState`, `siteConfig.minTlsVersion`, `siteConfig.cors.allowedOrigins`, and the two `basicPublishingCredentialsPolicies` child resources (`ftp` and `scm`). The debug page is switched on by an app setting.
