For sign-in, look up **"Configure your App Service app to use Microsoft Entra sign-in"** in the Azure docs. In the portal it's **Settings > Authentication > Add identity provider > Microsoft**. For an API, choose **Require authentication** and **HTTP 401 Unauthorized**. In Bicep it's a `Microsoft.Web/sites/config` resource named `authsettingsV2`, with `globalValidation.requireAuthentication: true` and `unauthenticatedClientAction: 'Return401'`. Then remember: this only proves **who** is calling. Your code must still decide which orders that caller may read.
