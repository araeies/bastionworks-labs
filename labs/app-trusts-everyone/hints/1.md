Start by looking, from outside, like the pen testers did. Open `http://<your portal>.azurewebsites.net/debug/config` and note everything it tells you, including the `deploymentToken`. Then request `/api/orders/1` and `/api/orders/2` on your API, with no sign-in. In a terminal, `curl -i -H "Origin: https://evil.example" https://<your API>.azurewebsites.net/api/orders/1` shows the CORS headers too. Write down what each response gives away: it's the start of your review.
